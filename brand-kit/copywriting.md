## Naming routes

Recommended: ORVECT. Combines orientation and vector. Pronounced OR-VEKT. Short, technical and open to future product scope.

Exploratory alternatives, not cleared: MOTRACE (motor + trace), VECTORA (vector + orientation), TORVIA (torque + way). These are brainstorming routes only; search availability and registration before adopting any name.

Prior explorations noted by the project lead: AXELITH has an existing software studio use, VEYDRIS an existing AI account use, and AXION is crowded in automotive. They are not recommended here. DiagPilot is the prior prototype identity.
